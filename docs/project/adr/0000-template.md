# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a decision

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context
What forces the decision: the rule, the risk or the constraint. Two to five sentences.

## Decision
What we do. Concrete enough to check in code.

## Rejected alternatives
- **Alternative:** why not.

## Consequences
What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what to do when the context changes.
